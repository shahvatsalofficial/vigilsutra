## Response targets

| Step | Target |
|---|---|
| Acknowledge a private report | 7 days |
| Confirm or reject | 14 days |
| Fix or mitigation for a confirmed issue | best effort, usually within 30 days |
| Public advisory after the fix ships | with credit to the reporter unless they ask otherwise |

This is a volunteer project; targets are goals, not guarantees. Whether they are met will be visible here.
