# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

- Be respectful and constructive. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated.
- Assume good faith; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Do not share other people's private information.
- Security research must stay within authorization: do not post exploits against systems you do not own.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through a GitHub private message or the repository's security advisory form. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect the community.
